title: Example calendars
---

> [!info] The example calendars can be copied to your vault directly.
> There is only one frontmatter property used in Gantt calendars. It defaults to `gantt-calendar-definition`.
> You may have to rename this property when copying these calendars to your vault.

All example calendars include inline comments for guidance.
Comments are everything behind the `'#'` symbol.
You may safely remove everything from the `'#'` symbol onward.

- [[gregorian-cal-no-year-zero|Gregorian Calendar without Year Zero]] - The global standard calendar everybody knows and uses.
- [[gregorian-calendar|Gregorian Calendar]] - The same calendar, but with year zero. Used in astronomy and computing.
- [[mayan-calendar|Mayan Calendar]] - The one that ended in 2012.
- [[dsa-ttrpg-calendar|DSA (TTRPG) Calendar]] - A classic fantasy world calendar.

For this plugin, all calendars are day-based; there is no time of day support.
This may change later, see [[roadmap|Roadmap]] for planned features.
